Q17 What does Dan say is important for conservation?
考点位置：选项
正确答案：B
B. picking a limited amount of mushrooms ✅
原文依据：You should never pick all the mushrooms in one area – collect only enough for your own needs.
讲解备注：数量限定词 +同替
 can't ... all + only enough for your own needs = a limited amount
考点：同义替换（解释型）
A. selecting only fully grown mushrooms ❌
原文依据：Be very careful that you don’t trample on young mushrooms or other plants.
讲解备注：只是说 don't trample on 别踩到， 不符合选项描述 跟only没啥关系的
考点：❌排除依据：仅提及，不符合 选项 限定
C. avoiding areas where rare mushroom species grow ❌
原文依据：And make sure you don’t pick any mushrooms that are endangered and protected by law.
讲解备注：And make sure you don’t = avoid
但是 避免的事 不要pick， 而不是说这个area不能去， 偷换概念
考点：排除依据(考点在选项)： 偷换概念

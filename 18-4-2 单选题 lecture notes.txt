Museum of Farming Life
题型：Part 2 单选题

Q11 The museum building was originally
考点位置：题干 / 时间
正确答案：B
B. a private home. ✅
原文依据：as the home of a local businessman
讲解备注：home = private home
考点：同义替换（近义词/平行替换）
A. a factory. ❌
原文依据：Alfred Palmer, of the Palmer biscuit factory.
讲解备注：这里介绍的是商人的「工厂」背景，并没有说博物馆建筑原来是工厂。
考点：❌排除依据: 仅提及 不符合题干限定
C. a hall of residence. ❌
原文依据：later …
讲解备注：不符合 originally。
考点：❌排除依据: 仅提及 不符合题干限定
讲题路径：先问：originally 要找的是哪个时间点？
再问：factory 是这座建筑的用途，还是 Palmer 的背景？
最后用 later 对比“最初”和“后来”，排除 C。
讲解提醒：factory 所属信息 ≠ 建筑原用途；later 切换了时间范围。

Q12 The university uses part of the museum building as
考点位置：题干
正确答案：A
C. administration offices. ❌
原文依据：apart from two rooms that are our offices
讲解备注：our 指博物馆；需核对使用主体。apart from 划定排除范围。
考点：❌排除依据: 仅提及 不符合题干限定
A. teaching rooms. ✅
原文依据：for lessons = teaching rooms
考点：同义替换（近义词/平行替换）
B. a research library. ❌
原文依据：[Q12干扰项（B） 只是说这个房间内饰好] It’s a shame because the interior architectural features are outstanding, especially the room that used to be the library.
讲解备注：你提供的整理：只说房间内饰好，和大学使用它的用途无关。待补完整原文。
考点：❌排除依据: 仅提及 不符合题干限定
讲题路径：先问：这道题问的是谁在用这些房间？
听到 our offices，our 指大学还是博物馆？
再把 for lessons 和 teaching rooms 对上。
提醒：library 只出现在内饰介绍中，不能推断现用途。
讲解提醒：讲解校准：排除 C 的核心是使用主体不同（博物馆的办公室 ≠ 大学用途），并非“只有两间”不符合 part of。

Q13 What does the guide say about the entrance fee?
考点位置：选项
正确答案：A
B. Only children and students receive a discount. ❌
原文依据：keep entrance to museum free
考点：❌排除依据：仅提及，不符合 选项 限定
C. The museum charges extra for special exhibitions. ❌
原文依据：run activities + no charge
讲解备注：no 否定收费。此片段是你的记录，尚需核对 special exhibitions 的直接依据。
考点：❌否定排除 - 否定信号词
A. Visitors decide whether or not they wish to pay. ✅
原文依据：feel free to give whatever amount
考点：同义替换（解释型）
讲题路径：entrance fee 用来定位；具体判断要看三个选项。
先问：免费和“只有某些人打折”一样吗？
再问：no charge 能支持 charges extra 吗？
最后核对：付费是否由参观者自己决定？（需补原文确认）
讲解提醒：待核对：你提供的 C 依据是 activities + no charge；需补完整原文确认与 special exhibitions 的对应范围。A 的直接原文也待补。

Q14 What are visitors advised to leave in the cloakroom?
考点位置：题干
正确答案：C
A. cameras ❌
原文依据：unlike … you might like to keep
讲解备注：与其他博物馆不同，这里可以留在身边使用。
C. bags ✅
原文依据：You might be more comfortable not carrying around heavy rucksacks.
讲解备注：rucksacks = bags；
not carrying =leave
考点：同义替换（近义词/平行替换）；同义替换（反向&否定）
B. coats ❌
原文依据：though keep your coats and jackets on
讲解备注：keep … on 是继续穿着，与 leave in the cloakroom 相反。
考点：❌否定排除 - 语义
讲题路径：先问：leave 在这里是“离开”，还是“把东西留在某处”？
keep cameras 与 leave 的方向相同吗？
not carrying heavy rucksacks 对应哪个选项？
最后问：keep your coats on 是穿着，还是存放？
讲解提醒：这里依据语义判断“建议留下什么”；keep … on 与 leave in the cloakroom 构成语义上的对立。

原文：
(Q11（单选）) Good morning everyone, and welcome to the Museum of Farming Life.

I understand it’s your first visit here, so I’d like to give you some background information about the museum and then explain a little about what you can see during your visit.

[Q11答案句（B） + Q11干扰项（A）] So, where we’re standing at the moment is the entrance to a large building that was constructed in 1880 as the home of a local businessman, Alfred Palmer, of the Palmer biscuit factory.

[Q11干扰项（ C） 不符合 题干 originally] It was later sold and became a hall of residence for students in 1911, and a museum in 1951. In 2005, a modern extension was built to accommodate the museum’s collections.

(Q12) The museum’s owned by the university, and apart from two rooms that are our offices, the university uses the main part of the building.

[Q12答案句（A） for lessons= teaching rooms] You may see students going into the building for lessons, but it’s not open to museum visitors, I’m afraid.

[Q12干扰项（B） 只是说这个房间内饰好] It’s a shame because the interior architectural features are outstanding, especially the room that used to be the library.

(Q13) Luckily, we’ve managed to keep entry to the museum free.

[Q13干扰项，排除B，  不是 only 小孩和学生] This includes access to all the galleries, outdoor areas and the rooms for special exhibitions.

[Q13干扰项，排除C， no charge] We run activities for children and students, such as the museum club, for which there’s no charge.

[Q13答案句（A）] We do have a donation box just over there so feel free to give whatever amount you consider appropriate.

(Q14) We do have a cloakroom, if you’d like to leave your coats and bags somewhere.

[Q14干扰项A，排除依据： unlike别的， camera 我们这里可以keep] Unlike other museums, photography is allowed here, so you might like to keep your cameras with you.

[Q14答案句（C） rucksack = bag] You might be more comfortable not carrying around heavy rucksacks, though keep your coats and jackets on as it’s quite cold in the museum garden today.

(Q15（Matching）) [四季动画与乡村厨房] I’d like to tell you about the different areas of the museum.

Just inside, and outside the main gallery, we have an area called Four Seasons.

Here you can watch a four-minute animation of a woodland scene. It was designed especially for the museum by a group of young people on a film studies course, and it’s beautiful.

Children absolutely love it, but then, so do adults.

The main gallery’s called Town and Country.

It includes a photographic collection of prizewinning sheep and shepherds. Leaving Town and Country, you enter Farmhouse Kitchen, which is … well, self-explanatory.

Here we have the oldest collection of equipment for making butter and cheese in the country.

And this morning, a specialist cheesemaker will be giving demonstrations of how it’s produced.

You may even get to try some.

[楼上观景与农场记忆测试] After that, you can go in two directions.

To the right is a staircase that takes you up to a landing from where you can look down on the galleries.

To the left is a room called A Year on the Farm.

There’s lots of seating here as sometimes we use the room for school visits, so it’s a good place to stop for a rest.

If you’re feeling competitive, you can take our memory test in which you answer questions about things you’ve seen in the museum.

[农用车展区与参观路线] The next area’s called Wagon Walk. This contains farm carts from nearly every part of the country.

It’s surprising how much regional variation there was.

Beside the carts are display boards with information about each one.

The carts are old and fragile, so we ask you to keep your children close to you and ensure they don’t climb on the carts.

From Wagon Walk, you can either make your way back to reception or go out into the garden – or even go back to take another look in the galleries.

[花园、蜂蜜与池塘] In the far corner of the garden is Bees are Magic, but we’re redeveloping this area so you can’t visit that at the moment.

You can still buy our honey in the shop, though.

Finally, there’s The Pond, which contains all kinds of interesting wildlife.

There are baby ducks that are only a few days old, as well as tiny frogs.

The Pond isn’t deep and there’s a fence around it, so it’s perfectly safe for children.

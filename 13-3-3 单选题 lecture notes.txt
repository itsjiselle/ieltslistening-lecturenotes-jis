Project on using natural dyes to colour fabrics

Q21 What first inspired Jim to choose this project?
考点位置：题干 / 时间
正确答案：C
A. textiles displayed in an exhibition ❌
原文依据：[Q21干扰项A：听转折] JIM: Well, I got a lot of useful ideas from the museum, you know, at that exhibition of textiles.&nbsp;
C. carpets he saw on holiday ✅
原文依据：But I've always been interested in anything to do with colour. Years ago, I went to a carpet shop with my parents when we were on holiday in Turkey, and I remember all the amazing colours.&nbsp;

Q24 What problem did Jim have with using tartrazine as a fabric dye?
考点位置：选项
正确答案：B
B. It was not a permanent dye on cotton. ✅
原文依据：[Q24答案句B：wash之后，color就 wash away, 🟰 not a permanent dye] JIM: Yeah. I used it on cotton first. It came out a great colour, but when I rinsed the material, the colour just washed away.
讲解备注：rinse + wash away = not permanent
考点：同义替换（反向&否定）
C. It was ineffective when used on nylon. ❌
原文依据：[Q24干扰项C： nylon， 想用。 Jim自己排除： I abandon] I'd been going to try it out on nylon, but I abandoned that idea.
讲解备注：否定语义： I abandoned that
考点：❌排除依据：仅提及，不符合 选项 限定；❌否定排除 - 语义

原文：
(Q21（单选）) TUTOR: OK, Jim. You wanted to see me about your textile design project.

JIM: That's right. I've been looking at how a range of natural dyes can be used to colour fabrics like cotton and wool.&nbsp;

TUTOR: Why did you choose that topic?

[Q21干扰项A：听转折] JIM: Well, I got a lot of useful ideas from the museum, you know, at that exhibition of textiles.&nbsp;

[Q21答案句C：听转折] But I've always been interested in anything to do with colour. Years ago, I went to a carpet shop with my parents when we were on holiday in Turkey, and I remember all the amazing colours.&nbsp;

TUTOR: They might not all have been natural dyes.

[Q21干扰项B： 是在决定follow it up 之后才找来的书。] JIM: Maybe not, but for the project I decided to follow it up. And I found a great book about a botanic garden in California that specialises in plants used for dyes.&nbsp;

(Q22) TUTOR: OK. So, in your project, you had to include a practical investigation.

JIM: Yeah. At first I couldn't decide on my variables. I was going to just look at one type of fibre for example, like cotton ...&nbsp;

TUTOR: ... and see how different types of dyes affected it?

JIM: Yes. Then I decided to include others as well, so I looked at cotton and wool and nylon.&nbsp;

TUTOR: With just one type of dye?

[Q22答案句：既提到了one dye, one fibre, 最后确认 都是好几种] JIM: Various types, including some that weren't natural, for comparison.

TUTOR: OK.

(Q23) [Q23干扰项B： 网站有supply，不代表 widely available，偷换概念] JIM: So, I did the experiments last week. I used some ready-made natural dyes, I found a website which supplied them, they came in just a few days, but I also made some of my own.&nbsp;

TUTOR: That must have taken quite a bit of time.

[Q23答案句A：原本以为只要 a teaspoon] JIM: Yes, I'd thought it'd just be a matter of a teaspoon or so of dye, and actually that wasn't the case at all. Like I was using one vegetable, beetroot, for a red dye, and I had to chop up a whole pile of it.&nbsp;

[Q23干扰项C： 费时间的是切东西，不是选项说的 leave the fabric in the dye] So it all took longer than I'd expected.&nbsp;

(Q24) TUTOR: One possibility is to use food colourings.

JIM: I did use one. That was a yellow dye, an artificial one.&nbsp;

TUTOR: Tartrazine?

[Q24答案句B：wash之后，color就 wash away, 🟰 not a permanent dye] JIM: Yeah. I used it on cotton first. It came out a great colour, but when I rinsed the material, the colour just washed away.&nbsp;

[Q24干扰项C： nylon， 想用。 Jim自己排除： I abandon] I'd been going to try it out on nylon, but I abandoned that idea.

TUTOR: Were you worried about health issues?

JIM: I'd thought if it's a legal food colouring, it must be safe. Jim：

[Q24干扰项A： 有过敏反应， 本身没问题，但不是Jim的problem。 这句话是导师说的。 题干说Jim， 就听Jim说的内容] TUTOR: Well, it can occasionally cause allergic reactions, I believe.

(Q25（Matching）) TUTOR: So what natural dyes did you look at?

JIM: Well, one was turmeric. The colour's great, it's a really strong yellow. It's generally used in dishes like curry.&nbsp;

[Q25答案句： not permanent = not long-lasting] TUTOR: It's meant to be quite good for your health when eaten, but you might find it's not permanent when it's used as a dye - a few washes, and it's gone.

(Q26) [Q26答案句：F 颜色unexpected， 手变red, 羊毛变 watery cream， + 态度 disappointed = unexpected] JIM: Right. I used beetroot as a dye for wool. When I chop up beetroot to eat I always end up with bright red hands, but the wool ended up just a sort of watery cream shade. Disappointing.&nbsp;

(Q27) TUTOR: There's a natural dye called Tyrian purple. Have you heard of that?

[Q27干扰项A： expensive？ 只说过去身份显赫的用，不代表现在还 expensive] JIM: Yes. It comes from a shellfish, and it was worn in ancient times but only by important people as it was so rare. I didn't use it.&nbsp;

[Q27答案句H： 有人manage to得到了一些， = not generally available] TUTOR: It fell out of use centuries ago, though one researcher managed to get hold of some recently. But that shade of purple can be produced by chemical dyes nowadays.&nbsp;

(Q28) Did you use any black dyes?

JIM: Logwood. That was quite complicated. I had to prepare the fabric so the dye would take.

TUTOR: I hope you were careful to wear gloves.

JIM: Yes. I know the danger with that dye.&nbsp;

[Q28答案句D： poisonous 解释型] TUTOR: Good. It can be extremely dangerous if it's ingested.&nbsp;

(Q29) Now, presumably you had a look at an insect-based dye? Like cochineal, for example?

[Q29干扰项H： 提到了not available, 只是这个地方not available, 不代表选项中说的generally] JIM: Yes. I didn't actually make that, I didn't have time to start crushing up insects to get the red colour and anyway they're not available here, but I managed to get the dye quite easily from a website.&nbsp;

[Q29答案句A： It is expensive.] But 【it cost a fortune】. I can see why it's generally just used in cooking, and in small quantities.&nbsp;

TUTOR: Yes, it's very effective, but that's precisely why it's not used as a dye.

(Q30) JIM: I also read about using metal oxide. Apparently you can allow iron to rust while it's in contact with the fabric, and that colours it.

[Q30答案句E：It can damage the fabric, damage = affect] TUTOR: Yes, that works well for dying cotton. But you have to be careful as the metal 【can actually affect the fabric】 and so you can't expect to get a lot of wear out of fabrics treated in this way.&nbsp;

[Q30干扰项F： unexpected， 颜色subtle不代表就是unexpected] And the colours are quite subtle, not everyone likes them. Anyway, it looks as if you've done a lot of work ...
